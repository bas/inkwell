# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual users managing personal Markdown notes on macOS. They need a calm, reliable workspace to capture, edit, search, and organize notes without file-format lock-in.

## Product Purpose

Inkwell is a local-first Markdown notes app with a WYSIWYG editor and plain-file storage. It exists to make personal note-taking fast and dependable while keeping notes portable as `.md` files. Success means users can create, find, and maintain notes with minimal friction and high trust in data ownership.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, and dependable. The interface should feel calm and task-oriented, with clear hierarchy and low visual noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy, marketing-heavy visuals, novelty UI patterns, and decorative interactions that distract from writing and retrieval tasks.

## Design Principles

1. Keep writing flow uninterrupted by prioritizing clarity and low-friction interactions.
2. Preserve user trust with predictable behavior and transparent, local-first data handling.
3. Favor familiar, accessible UI patterns over custom or surprising affordances.
4. Maintain visual restraint so content remains the primary focus.
5. Design for quick capture and quick recall on every primary screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support reduced motion preferences and maintain strong text/background contrast throughout core workflows.
